## Question and falsification

Can randomly initialized, local-observation self-play policies learn useful fixed-spawn rifle dueling within a separately recorded 12-hour wall-clock allowance? Primary estimand: the mean, across independently trained seeds 5101/5102/5103, of final minus initial benchmark round score. Win/draw/loss score is 1/0.5/0, equally weighted across the three opponents and two spawn ends. Hypothesis: improvement >=0.20. A measured improvement below 0.20 fails this numerical hypothesis; a confidence interval spanning it is inconclusive about the population effect. Do not manufacture significance by extending runs or replacing weak seeds. Three models are an initial experiment, not a guarantee of adequate statistical power [COLAS18, AGARWAL21].

Engineering acceptance (approved thresholds, not literature-derived optima): median final model score >=0.70 against random and corresponding initial policies, >=0.55 against the moving scripted benchmark, and >=90% of all benchmark rounds resolved by elimination. Fixed layouts support no claim about random spawns, unseen maps or general tactical skill.

## Frozen game and model

`src/ministrike/sim/specs.py`: `duel-fixed-v1`, map `flat14-fixed6-v1`, two players, one per team. Flat 14 x 14 metre interior, four perimeter walls, no internal geometry. Feet at (0,0,-3) and (0,0,3), yaw 0/pi, pitch zero. Learner spawn alternates per arena after each round. No spawn jitter, objectives, pickups or respawns. 20 Hz, 400-tick (20 s) horizon. Both shots resolve before elimination; one survivor wins; zero survivors draw; two survivors at timeout draw. Health100, damage25, 30/90 ammunition, configured6shots/s with tick overshoot retained, 2s reload, 5m/s movement. These are user requirements/retained settings, not empirically optimized choices.

Actor: personal/public geometry prefix plus two visibility-masked player rows (48 features), four frames (192 inputs), 128-tanh-128-tanh, factorized categorical heads (9,5,5,2,2). Movement, yaw, pitch, fire and reload are learned; interaction is absent from the distribution and entropy. Physical seven-component controls retain an inactive interaction field for the shared simulation. A 110-degree 3D field of view and wall LOS mask enemy positions; enemy health is never exposed. Own state and public team identity remain visible. No aiming or navigation assistance. Critic: own learner team's combined observations, 256-tanh-256-tanh, one value per learner. For 1v1 this is a separate value network with the same information as the actor. Initialization is PyTorch default random initialization, independently seeded; no pretrained or diagnostic weights.

Algorithm: TorchRL 0.14 `EnvBase.rollout`, GAE and `ClipPPOLoss`; PyTorch Adam. Our adapter, masks and orchestration surround library losses [TORCHRL]. Adam3e-4, PPOclip0.2, 3epochs, 128tickrollouts, minibatch256 arena-time rows, gamma0.999, lambda0.95, entropy0.01, critic coefficient0.5, smooth-L1 value loss, gradient norm limit1.0. Normalize advantages over live-before-action slots, after temporal GAE; mask dead actor/entropy terms only. Round termination is distinct from collection boundaries. All values are registered engineering choices or retained project settings.

Each arena has one learner and one frozen opponent. Choose at round reset with 50% probability latest snapshot, otherwise uniformly from initial plus up to eight rolling historical candidates. Snapshot each ten completed updates. Retain active retired snapshots until their rounds finish; never sample those as extra historical candidates. Preserve initial forever. Seeds have independent models, optimizers, RNGs, pools and simulator state. Scripted controllers are evaluation/test fixtures only; never training opponents, targets or initialization data [SF20 is inspiration, not a reproduced algorithm].

## Reward candidates and pilot selection

Terminal win+1/loss-1/draw0. Add `alpha*(effective_damage_dealt-effective_damage_received)/100`, capping total damage to a victim's remaining health. R0 alpha0.25; R1 alpha0.50; R2 alpha0.50 plus `gamma*Phi(next)-Phi(current)`. Phi is `0.1*max(0,cos(theta))` for the visible unobstructed enemy's body centre; hidden and terminal potential zero. The discounted telescoping identity is tested, including terminal transitions [NG99]. This identity is not a convergence guarantee for self-play with partial observations. No separate movement/survival/firing/reload/ammunition rewards.

All exploratory pilots use seed5201 and the identical initial actor hash; equal arena-step targets, algorithm, opponent rules and architecture. Start R0. Retain if primary validation score improves >=0.10 and correctness passes. Otherwise diagnose first, then R1, then R2 if needed. A qualifying candidate's higher validation score wins; differences <=0.02 prefer the simpler reward. Each reward has a separate experiment/run ID. If no candidate qualifies, do not start confirmation: preserve negatives and register a specific diagnosis within the remaining allowance. Do not spend the remaining budget on unspecified variants. A failed run is not a replacement opportunity.

It is created only after published-source preflight; the clock begins immediately before the first optimizer-bearing experiment (including throughput calibration), conservatively including initialization and collection. Absolute deadline is start+12h; it cannot be reset by resume, process restart or a new run directory. Approximately first2h calibration/pilots, next8h confirmation, last2h final evaluation/reporting. All interruptions, diagnostics and checkpoint writes count. No Stage A work restarts the old 5v5 suite.

Reuse the ten historical hardware probes [evidence index]; their winner is a starting point, not assumed optimal for two players. Bounded new comparison: optimizer CUDA, inference CPU or CUDA, 16 or64 arenas, 1 or3 concurrent independent learners, one CPU thread per learner. Eight cells; four completed updates per learner per cell, <=45s per cell, total <=12min including launch/serialization. Also include one CPU-optimizer/CPU-inference/16-arena/single-learner reference cell under the same bound. All use seed5290+worker, R0 and scratch initialization; diagnostic weights never enter pilots or confirmation. Compare **complete** collection/update/checkpoint throughput, not GPU-utilization percentage. Record GPU memory/utilization and host memory/CPU. Reject failures/nonfinite results or memory headroom <20%; retain the evidence. Choose maximum measured aggregate arena-step/s; if within5%, prefer lower memory and fewer processes. Freeze result before pilots; no repeat calibration without a dated failure amendment.

Freeze pilot target as a multiple of `num_envs*128`: `floor(0.8 * single_run_throughput * 1200 / batch_frames) * batch_frames`, at least one batch. Each candidate has <=20min training, <=10min validation/diagnosis. Account for remaining first2h allowance before launching; never shorten one candidate's step budget to make it comparable to a larger earlier one. Serial execution uses the same aggregate formula; each of three models receives one third of available training wall time. Every model gets exactly the same target. Record resolved values, config hashes, selected reward and power analysis in a committed/pushed amendment before confirmation. Run fresh5101/5102/5103. Resume restores optimizer, RNGs, in-flight games, snapshot identities, frame/update counts and source identity; downtime still consumes the absolute deadline. Archive metrics newer than the last valid checkpoint before replaying an interrupted suffix.

## Frozen evaluation

Three baselines: reproducible categorical random policy, that model's corresponding frozen initial policy, and `DuelScriptedPolicy` moving combat benchmark. The script uses only the same local observations, the same five categorical heads and exact decoder (nearest quantized yaw/pitch); its public-map movement is deterministic. Freeze implementation before pilots. No human or scripted data reaches training.

Validation root3,000,000; final-test root4,000,000; training seeds5101-5103/pilot5201/calibration5290-5292 are disjoint. Evaluate both ends for every opponent. Primary deterministic deployment; secondary independently labeled stochastic actor evaluation. Use128 paired seeds for matches with stochastic policies; use the same opponent RNG seed for initial/final and side-swapped paired worlds. Fixed starts with two deterministic controllers produce only two unique side trajectories: record once per side, not128 pseudo-replicates. Reuse identical random controls/RNG root across checkpoints; policy interactions can diverge. Pair on baseline/side/seed during uncertainty resampling. Initial policy baseline is deterministic in primary evaluation and stochastic in secondary evaluation. Random policy is stochastic in both.

Freeze deployment choice by mean validation score, tie by lower training seed, **before any final-test evaluation**. Evaluate initial and final of all seeds on final test; publish all, not only the deployment winner. For each model report wins/losses/draws, side scores, elimination/timeouts, kills, damage, shots, hit accuracy, reload occupancy, aim saturation and inactivity. Report stochastic and deterministic analyses separately. Repeated fixed trajectories are not independent match samples.

Before confirmation publish noncentral-t power sensitivity for a two-sided paired t test, alpha0.05, n3 independent differences, effect0.20, assumed between-model SD0.05/0.10/0.20 [COLAS18]. This is an assumed normal-difference sensitivity calculation, not estimated power or an empirical guarantee. Report individual model deltas and a t interval with2df as a descriptive, assumption-sensitive interval; separate paired match-bootstrap intervals. Do not treat thousands of games as thousands of independently trained models [AGARWAL21].
