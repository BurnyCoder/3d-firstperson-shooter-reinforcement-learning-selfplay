## What the quantities mean

An **actor** maps permitted observations to action probabilities; the **critic** estimates expected future reward for training. An **MLP** is a feed-forward neural network; 2x128 means two hidden layers of 128 units. **PPO** limits the change in action probability through a clipped policy objective; **GAE** estimates temporal advantage. **MAPPO** applies the PPO pattern to multiple agents with a shared actor and centralized training critic. These definitions follow [the official TorchRL implementation](https://docs.pytorch.org/rl/stable/tutorials/multiagent_ppo.html) and [MAPPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01955).

**Self-play** here means learning against immutable snapshots of that run's policies; historical opponents change at round boundaries. A **seed** controls random initialization and sampling. The three confirmation seeds are independently trained models; 128 evaluation seeds are different matches with a particular frozen model. A **paired improvement** subtracts initial from final score under matched evaluation conditions. A confidence interval over matches does not measure uncertainty over retraining. [Evaluation reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264).

**Round score** is win 1 / draw 0.5 / loss 0. **Accuracy** is successful hits divided by actual shots, undefined when no shots occur. **Elimination fraction** includes wins, losses and simultaneous-kill draws, and excludes timeouts. **Inactivity** is the fraction of decisions where all decoded controls are zero; it does not detect every unproductive movement loop. **Fill** is not a meaningful shooter metric and is not reported. **Completion** refers to the registered arena-step target, not learning acceptance. An **arena step** is one shared simulation tick; Stage A has one learner decision and two total player decisions per step.

**Loss** is the optimizer's objective, not a score against a fixed opponent. **Reward graphs** are interval means per arena step, not cumulative reward or episode returns. Changing self-play opponents make training wins unsuitable as a common skill scale. All plots retain raw intervals, missingness and source hashes; see the [graph guide](../docs/experiment-plots.md).

## What was learned and what remains unresolved

Increasing damage shaping, adding potential shaping, altering inference sampling, training longer and lowering entropy did not solve the initial raw-coordinate recipe under their registered tests. Transforming already-visible relative positions into the actor's own coordinate frame yielded a large matched-seed gain without adding privileged state or scripted aiming. This supports that specific representation intervention in that exploratory setting; it does not prove it is optimal or alone sufficient for robust combat. The three fresh models reproduce gains against weak controls but fail the moving-script requirement. This negative confirmation is preserved rather than replacing weak seeds or selecting another checkpoint using test results.

All interruption/recovery, calibration, failed launch and learning-audit records remain linked through the [evidence index](../docs/evidence-index.md). The [public model archive](publication/README.md) contains experimental, diagnostic and deferred labels. Later random-spawn, tactical-map, variable-player, team and bomb stages have not run under this budget.
