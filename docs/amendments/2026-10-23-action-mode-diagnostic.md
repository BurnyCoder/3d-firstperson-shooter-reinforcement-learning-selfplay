## Question, hypothesis and controls

The recorded deterministic and stochastic initial-policy evaluations change both controllers' action-selection modes. Consequently their difference cannot isolate the learner's mode. Hold model weights, observations, rules, initial opponent, seeds and spawn ends fixed, and intervene independently on the two modes in a 2-by-2 factorial design. D means per-head argmax; S means sampling the factorized categorical distribution. The first letter is the learner, the second the opponent: DD, DS, SD, SS. TorchRL treats action selection as distinct from module train/eval mode; our existing evaluation helper uses explicit argmax or `torch.multinomial` with per-world generators. [TorchRL 0.14 sampling API](https://docs.pytorch.org/rl/stable/reference/generated/torchrl.envs.set_exploration_type.html), [PyTorch multinomial](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.multinomial.html).

Primary hypothesis, an **engineering diagnostic threshold**: the equally weighted mean over the three frozen R0/R1/R2 models of score(SD) minus score(DD) is at least **0.10**. This asks whether changing only the learner's mode materially affects score against the deterministic initial policy. A point estimate below 0.10 fails this diagnostic threshold; an interval spanning it limits confidence. No significance-based extension or threshold revision is permitted. This is not the original learning-improvement hypothesis and cannot qualify confirmation or deployment.

Secondary, fully reported contrasts for each reward: SD-DD, SS-DS, DS-DD and interaction `(SS-DS)-(SD-DD)`. The first two compare learner modes at a fixed opponent mode; the third isolates opponent mode at a fixed learner mode. Also report every cell's score, wins/draws/losses, both spawn ends, elimination, damage, shots/accuracy, reload, pitch saturation and inactivity. Keep negative and incomplete cells. The official [SB3 evaluation guidance](https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/guide/rl_tips.html) motivates testing action modes, but its observation that deterministic predictions often help is not a guarantee for this game and is not evidence for our hypothesis. SB3 is a documentation reference, not an added training dependency.

## Analysis, traces and failure interpretation

Score is win1/draw0.5/loss0. Average the two spawn ends within each common seed. Broadcast DD's known deterministic side average only as a constant reference in arithmetic; its raw records stay two. For the primary contrast, average the three reward-variant differences within each seed block before resampling. Thus common-seed coupling and both sides remain intact. Use SciPy 1.18.1 `stats.bootstrap`, 5,000 percentile resamples, 95% interval, NumPy generator seed5701. Secondary intervals use the same seed-block construction, are descriptive and are not multiplicity-adjusted significance tests. Three reward variants trained with the same seed are **not three independent trained-model replications**. These intervals condition on these fixed policies and this map. [SciPy API](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html), [evaluation uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264).

Trace only the prospectively fixed first world seed, 3,000,000, on each spawn end in every cell: 24 traces. Record pre-action positions, orientation, health, ammunition, reload/cooldown, decoded controls, legal visibility and visible-opponent body-centre angular error, plus actual shot/damage/termination events. Raw audit traces include both players' state; they are never actor inputs or training targets. Tracing must not mutate simulation or RNG, verified with a focused test. Inspect movement/aim loops, time with visible opponents, firing/reload requests and actual shots. A visible target or small centre-angle error alone is not proof that a ray would hit; authoritative damage events decide actual hits.

Planned artifacts: source/config/deadline receipt; all 12 raw cell results; all 24 traces; paired contrast JSON; Markdown report; score/contrast PNG+SVG; representative fixed-seed position/health trace figures for every model and end; shared report/PDF/evidence-index updates. The trace selection cannot be changed after viewing outcomes. Distinguish numerical behavioral summaries from post-hoc causal hypotheses.

If learner-only mode changes have little effect, the learner-mode explanation is weakened for this fixed opponent and task. A large effect establishes a narrow action-selection dependence, not the cause of poor scripted-opponent performance, an optimizer bug, convergence or an accepted model. Opponent-policy behavior can respond to changed learner observations: holding its mode and weights fixed does not hold every action fixed. Any subsequent reward, entropy, exploration, horizon or training-duration intervention needs its own prospective identity, control and compute bound; this registration authorizes none automatically.
