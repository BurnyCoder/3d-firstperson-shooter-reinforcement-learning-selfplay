## Questions and evidence motivating them

1. Does four times the pilot experience improve deterministic benchmark score when all learning settings stay fixed? Primary duration hypothesis: control score at update 608 minus its own update-152 score is at least **0.10**.
2. At equal experience, does reducing entropy regularization by tenfold improve deterministic benchmark score? Primary entropy hypothesis: low-entropy score at update 608 minus control score at update 608 is at least **0.10**.

A point estimate below its threshold fails that numerical hypothesis; an interval spanning it limits confidence. Report both results, every endpoint and all negatives; no significance-based extension. These are engineering thresholds for two specific mechanisms, not established literature optima. R1 is chosen because it had the highest primary score among the completed reward pilots; this is an explicitly post-result choice for a new registration, not evidence that R1 is globally optimal. The fixed traces show firing without reloading and wall-bound movement; they do not establish why training produced these behaviors.

Sample Factory's primary Doom work uses self-play, population diversity and PBT that changes entropy/reward parameters; its large experience budgets motivate testing duration separately. This project retains TorchRL PPO and its existing factorized MLP, with no PBT, external policy, scripted training or copied implementation. Doom's pixel observations, recurrent architecture, task, hardware and frame conventions differ, so neither its frame count nor runtime predicts convergence here. [Sample Factory paper, sections 3.5/4.3](https://vladlen.info/papers/sample-factory.pdf), [official PBT parameters](https://www.samplefactory.dev/07-advanced-topics/pbt/), [TorchRL PPO entropy coefficient](https://docs.pytorch.org/rl/stable/reference/generated/torchrl.objectives.ClipPPOLoss.html).
