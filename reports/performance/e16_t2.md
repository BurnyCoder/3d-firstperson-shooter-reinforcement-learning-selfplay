# Probe e16_t2

One self-play learner, seed 8002, 16 arenas, two PyTorch CPU threads, CUDA updates. Four measured updates after one warmup supplied 8,192 arena steps. Aggregate throughput was **791.59 steps/s** over 10.348838 seconds; the raw worker rate was 791.559219 steps/s. Group process wall time including startup and warmup was 23.906 seconds.

Collection used 81.79% and optimization 17.80% of the measured worker interval. This was the fastest 16-arena single-worker probe, but no concurrent two-thread group was measured. It does not establish that two threads are best when six workers share the CPU. Peak live PyTorch CUDA allocation was 156,522,496 bytes. The probe succeeded; no confidence interval or playing-quality conclusion is supported.
