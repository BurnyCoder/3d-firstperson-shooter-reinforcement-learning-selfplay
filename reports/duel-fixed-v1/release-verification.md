## Scope limits

The native session verified cameras, human firing, explicit human ownership, takeover/hand-back and replay equality. Remote WASD movement and mouse aiming were not successfully delivered; focused input-mapping tests cover them, but exhaustive physical playtesting is unverified. The real optimizer resume audit covers two updates, not every interruption case. Three seeds and two fixed trajectories per deterministic benchmark do not establish broad reliability or generalization. No later-stage training or final-test-driven tuning is authorized by this release.
